About

Thoughtful books begin with thoughtful partnership.

For ten years I was an acquiring editor at Island Press, where I worked with authors, scientists, and thought leaders to shape books that inform, inspire, and drive meaningful change.

Today, I partner with writers at every stage — refining proposals, strengthening manuscripts, and elevating language and structure for a powerful, lasting book.

I love working on books about nature, the environment, science, social impact, health, and the human experience — stories that help us understand our world and imagine a better one.

— Rebecca

Services

  • One-on-one guidance for writers shaping a book from the ground up — finding the spine of the idea, sketching a table of contents, and building a writing rhythm that survives a year of drafting. For writers between “I have a book in me” and a working outline.

    Often includes
    • Monthly working sessions
    • Structural feedback on early drafts
    • Accountability & check-ins

  • The book proposal is its own genre, and the document agents and acquiring editors actually read. I help you frame the argument, position the book against the market, sharpen sample chapters, and make every page do the work it needs to do. From a rough overview to a submission-ready package.

    Often includes
    • Overview & pitch
    • Comp titles & market framing
    • Annotated TOC + sample chapters

  • A reader's letter on what's working and what isn't. I read the manuscript the way an acquiring editor would — for argument, structure, voice and pace — and write you a candid editorial memo with the questions worth answering before you revise. No track changes, just clear thinking.

    Deliverable
    • 5–10 page editorial letter
    • Chapter-level notes
    • Follow-up call

  • Structure, argument, narrative arc — chapter by chapter. The deepest kind of edit: where chapters move, where threads need braiding, where the reader gets lost or pulls ahead. We work through several rounds until the book stands up on its own. For manuscripts that need shaping, not just polishing.

    Often includes
    • Manuscript-wide editorial letter
    • Inline structural notes
    • Two revision rounds

  • Sentence-level work for clarity, voice and rhythm. I read with an ear for the sentence underneath the sentence — tightening prose without flattening it, and querying anywhere a reader might trip. For manuscripts whose shape is settled and now need to sing.

    Deliverable
    • Tracked-changes Word doc
    • Margin queries & suggestions
    • Optional consistency pass

  • Jackets, catalogues, book proposals and the adjacent prose that sells a book before anyone reads it — flap copy, comp copy, author bios, the page on your website. For writers and small presses who want the words around the book to do justice to the book.

    Often includes
    • Jacket & catalogue copy
    • Author bios & About pages
    • Pitch language for press

  • Quotations, lyrics, archival images, long excerpts — the parts of a manuscript that quietly add risk and cost. I help authors map what needs permission, draft requests to rights holders, and plan around the asks most likely to bog the book down. A conversation, not a legal opinion.

    Often includes
    • Permissions inventory
    • Drafting rights requests
    • Cost & timeline planning
