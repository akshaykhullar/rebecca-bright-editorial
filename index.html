Meet the editor

A reader first, an editor second — and a quiet collaborator on the work in between.

I'm a freelance editor specializing in nonfiction with impact, from popular science and memoir to guidebooks and comics. For 10 years, I was an acquiring editor at Island Press, where I collaborated with award-winning authors to bring their passion and knowledge onto the page.

That decade taught me how a book is really made: not at the keyboard alone, but in the long conversations between writer and reader. I'm interested in the architecture under a chapter, the cadence of a paragraph, and the small, careful choices that make ideas land.

I work best with writers who care about craft — researchers translating their work for general readers, memoirists looking for shape and restraint, journalists building their first long-form, comics artists braiding image and text. Whatever the form, my goal is the same: to help you write the book you actually mean to write.
