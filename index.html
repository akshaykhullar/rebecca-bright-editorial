About

Thoughtful books begin with thoughtful partnership.

For ten years I was an acquiring editor at Island Press, where I worked with authors, scientists, and thought leaders to shape books that inform, inspire, and drive meaningful change.

Today, I partner with writers at every stage: refining proposals, strengthening manuscripts, and elevating language and structure for a powerful, lasting book.

I love working on books about nature, the environment, science, social impact, health, and the human experience: stories that help us understand our world and imagine a better one.

- Rebecca

Services

  • One-on-one guidance for writers shaping a book from the ground up: finding the spine of the idea, sketching a table of contents, and building a writing rhythm that survives a year of drafting.

    Often includes

    • Monthly working sessions
    • Structural feedback on early drafts
    • Accountability and check-ins

  • The book proposal is its own genre, and the document agents and acquiring editors actually read. I help frame the argument, position the book against the market, and sharpen sample chapters.

    Often includes

    • Overview and pitch
    • Comp titles and market framing
    • Annotated TOC and sample chapters

  • A reader's letter on what's working and what isn't. I read the manuscript the way an acquiring editor would: for argument, structure, voice, and pace.

    Deliverable

    • 5-10 page editorial letter
    • Chapter-level notes
    • Follow-up call

  • Structure, argument, narrative arc: chapter by chapter. The deepest kind of edit, where chapters move, threads braid, and the reader's path becomes clearer.

    Often includes

    • Manuscript-wide editorial letter
    • Inline structural notes
    • Two revision rounds

  • Sentence-level work for clarity, voice, and rhythm. I read with an ear for the sentence underneath the sentence: tightening prose without flattening it.

    Deliverable

    • Tracked-changes Word doc
    • Margin queries and suggestions
    • Optional consistency pass

  • Jackets, catalogues, book proposals, and the adjacent prose that sells a book before anyone reads it: flap copy, comp copy, author bios, and web copy.

    Often includes

    • Jacket and catalogue copy
    • Author bios and About pages
    • Pitch language for press
