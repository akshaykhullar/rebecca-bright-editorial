About Me

For ten years I was an acquiring editor at Island Press, where I worked with authors, scientists, and thought leaders to shape books that inform, inspire, and drive meaningful change.

Today, I partner with writers at every stage: refining proposals, strengthening manuscripts, and elevating language and structure for a powerful, lasting book.

I love working on books about nature, the environment, science, social impact, health, and the human experience: stories that help us understand our world and imagine a better one.

- Rebecca

Services

Every project is a little different. These are the core ways I work with writers, and each engagement can be tailored to the manuscript, timeline, and level of support you need. Pricing depends on scope; after I learn more about the book, I can suggest the right fit.

  • One-on-one guidance for writers shaping a book from the ground up: finding the spine of the idea, sketching a table of contents, building a writing rhythm, and staying accountable through the long middle of a project.

  • The book proposal is its own genre, and the document agents and acquiring editors actually read. I help frame the argument, position the book against the market, sharpen sample chapters, and make the project easier to understand at a glance.

  • A reader's letter on what's working and what isn't. I read the manuscript the way an acquiring editor would: for argument, structure, voice, pacing, audience, and the questions a reader may bring to the page.

  • Structure, argument, narrative arc: chapter by chapter. The deepest kind of edit, where chapters move, threads braid, and the reader's path through the book becomes clearer.

  • Sentence-level work for clarity, voice, and rhythm. I read with an ear for the sentence underneath the sentence: tightening prose, smoothing transitions, and strengthening the writing without flattening it.

  • A final polish for spelling, grammar, punctuation, consistency, and small errors that can distract a reader. This is best for work that has already been edited and is close to publication or submission.

  • Support for the prose around a book and, when needed, the prose inside it: jacket copy, catalogue copy, author bios, web copy, pitch language, and collaborative drafting that helps an idea find its clearest form.
